The first public release of Forage: YouTube without an account and without the feed.

**Two versions, one app:** *Forage* (Standard) for most people, and *Forage Full*, whose private
vault can also use Pornhub. Installing one over the other switches versions and keeps your library.

**Install:** on Android (15 or newer), download `forage-0.21.0.apk` (or `forage-full-0.21.0.apk`)
and open it. On Linux with `apt` (Debian, Ubuntu, Pop!_OS), download `forage_0.21.0_amd64.deb` (or
`forage-full_0.21.0_amd64.deb`) and open it, or run `sudo apt install ./forage_0.21.0_amd64.deb`.

**What's in it**

- Recommendations only when you ask, from what you like, each with the reason it was picked; quick
  picks right away while the recommendation model downloads in the background.
- History, likes, playlists, watch later, subscriptions with their new uploads, and where you left
  off in each video.
- Import from Google Takeout, NewPipe, FreeTube or LibreTube, with a welcome that walks you through it.
- On Android, videos play in Forage without ads. On Linux, mpv set up in one click (mpv, yt-dlp and
  Deno), FreeTube or the browser.
- Sync between phones, tablets and computers over your Wi-Fi, encrypted, with no server.
- Backups and exports; an encrypted private vault, hidden until unlocked.
- An optional check for new versions, off unless you switch it on.

**Upgrading from a build you were given before:** those were Full; install `forage-full` over
them and your library stays (Standard works too, without the vault's site). The update check is
offered once on Home.

SHA-256 checksums of all four files are in `SHA256SUMS`.
